## What to find
Search for concrete vendor openings and business-development leads for Scoops by the Sea: festivals, fairs, markets, food-truck events, concerts, municipal/county recreation, sports, tournaments, races, schools, PTO/PTA, churches, nonprofits, fundraisers, golf, HOAs, residential communities, resorts, campgrounds, employers, parks, museums, holiday events and private venues.

Use official organizer/government/application sources first. Also use chambers, tourism calendars, Eventeny/vendor platforms, credible local news and publicly accessible organizer social posts.

Do not treat a generic event listing as proof that food trucks are accepted. Expired applications, old forms and visitor admission prices are not current vendor opportunities.

Event/opportunity name, organizer, location, county/city, approximate mileage from Malacompra, distance tag, event date/hours, deadline, vendor fee, application/contact instructions, public contact info, food-truck acceptance evidence, dessert restrictions/exclusivity, known frozen-dessert competition, food-truck count if known, service hours, generator/power rules, insurance/license requirements, credible attendance evidence, cancellation/weather terms, source/date checked and a concrete next action.

- Verified Opportunity

## Profit-potential tags
Every legitimate opportunity should display HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW or UNKNOWN PROFIT POTENTIAL, with concrete reason tags such as LOW FEE, HIGH FEE, BIG CROWD, FAMILY CROWD, SPORTS TRAFFIC, LOCAL / LOW TRAVEL COST, REPEAT POTENTIAL, PREPAID POSSIBLE, MINIMUM GUARANTEE POSSIBLE, LIMITED DESSERT COMPETITION, DESSERT COMPETITION, LONG DRIVE, WEATHER RISK, PLACEMENT UNKNOWN or ATTENDANCE UNKNOWN.

Do not use a numerical profit score and do not hide legitimate lower-rated opportunities. Profit potential is a practical screening tag, not a forecast.

Compare with existing data and avoid duplicates. Update an existing event when its date, deadline, fee, application, opening, cancellation, reopening or other material fact changes. Verified items belong in event data; promising unverified items belong in Discovery Inbox.

Preserve Mike's local notes, pipeline stages, bookings and actual results. Never overwrite user-entered business history with scraped data.

At the end of each run, verify the site data still loads. Summarize new verified opportunities, material updates, strong leads, home-turf opportunities, 14-day deadlines, HIGH profit-potential items, retained >40-mile opportunities, source failures and the most important next contacts.
